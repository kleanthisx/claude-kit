"Work exactly like the other site" = functional parity: same controls, options, click results, combinations, persistence, empty states. NOT downloading the reference's pictures/data or making our content, counts and orderings match.

**Why:** 2026-10-02, a site clone vs its reference site, parity work: my agent brief allowed "local copies of the reference's static assets", and the six agents went off chasing assets and numbers. Operator: "they are trying to recreate the pages by getting assets to match the numbers. this is about functionality, not downloading assets." The day before the operator had also said six hours on one page was too slow - asset/data chasing is where the time goes.

**How to apply:** In any parity brief, state "functionality only; content differences are not defects". Don't record ranking/order/count drift as work items. Related: [[verify-ui-with-real-input]], [[a-given-task-is-not-reasked]].

> project. The lesson, the numbers and the user's words are unchanged.*

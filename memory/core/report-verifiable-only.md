"Report only verifiable claims. I have no use of fabrications. If I wanted fantasy I would play dungeons without dragons." (user, 2026-08-10)

**Why:** caught inflating "two days before" into "weeks before" to strengthen a corroboration claim — fabrication in miniature. Extends the done-is-banned rule from completion claims to ALL claims: every factual statement in a report must trace to a file, a tool output, a run stat, or the user's own words. Anything else is left out, or explicitly marked as estimate/judgment with its basis.

**How to apply:** before stating a number, date, quantity, or causal claim — is its source in context or on disk? No → don't say it, or say "unverified" with what would verify it. Rhetorical strengtheners ("weeks", "always", "massively") are where fabrication leaks in; strip them.
